# Made of Maybe — full-song optical Life karaoke (video renderer)

## Scope

We now have the *complete* preloaded B3/S23 karaoke universe generated by
`life.full_song_karaoke` from Suno's original alignment (468 source tokens,
403 sung words, 66 lines). The video renderer makes that giant world legible:

- `life/life_cell_sampler.py`: determine whether the **actual four live cells**
  of a 2×2 Life still-life exist at a given target position/generation. The
  sampler evaluates 40 real Life steps around both the **MAKE** and **ERASE**
  collisions. Elsewhere, it relies on the independently certified `c/4`
  glider flight and stable block persistence. All pixels of a given word
  have identical input phases but different spatial translations.
- `preview/render_full_song_karaoke.py`: render target live-cell occupancy,
  optically enlarged, in the physically placed word's **same left-to-right
  order**. A pan/zoom camera follows the current stage and latest word,
  while the other 65 lyric stages remain present in the universe but outside
  the camera's field of view. Video metadata/hud is merely presentational;
  the words themselves are never drawn by a font.
- The pipeline writes an **entire 238.88s, 24fps MP4**, with the music at its
  original 1× speed. No singer/bubble yet, per planned sequencing.

## What is—and is not—being rendered

The native Game of Life initial pattern is **not** compacted or changed.
The far-separated glider flight corridors are outside the video crop.
The optical projection divides each block's gigantic physical coordinates
by its verified pixel pitch (which is fixed within a lyric stage), then uses
an affine moving camera to keep the sung words readable. An active projected
pixel exists **if and only if all four actual target Life cells are alive**.

This is not a complete per-generation brute-force evolution of all 345,855
seed cells over 45,865 generations. Instead we use the previously qualified
packet physics plus exact B3/S23 local reaction windows, which are checked
against direct simulation by `tests.test_life_cell_sampler`. It follows
precisely from the noninteraction contract of the full-song compiler.

The singer/mouth are **not** yet rescheduled to 192 generations per second;
we will integrate those after the lyrics are visible and qualified.

## Full video via GitHub Actions

The lyric qualification workflow executes:

```sh
python -m unittest tests.test_life_cell_sampler -v
python preview/render_full_song_karaoke.py \
   --out output/lyric_cues/made_of_maybe_full_song_silent.mp4 \
   --thumbnail preview/full_song_karaoke_thumbnail.png \
   --metadata output/lyric_cues/full_song_video_provenance.json
```

Download the MP4 as part of the Actions artifact. Video is **silent** unless
an original audio file is supplied. We do *not* spend Suno export credits or
silently download audio. To combine with a legally obtained original WAV/MP3:

```sh
python preview/render_full_song_karaoke.py \
   --audio path/to/made_of_maybe.wav \
   --out output/made_of_maybe_conway_karaoke_with_audio.mp4
```

`--audio` muxes the original music; it does not change the audio speed.
For a short first-verse local sanity check, without needing the complete
Suno alignment in a separate clone:

```sh
python preview/render_full_song_karaoke.py --fixture --preview \
   --out output/first_two_lines_optical_teaser.mp4
```

## Objective checks and limitations

- **Exact native Life reactions:** the sampler's target-block truth table
  is compared directly against 302 Life generations, and separately with
  the first aligned word's actual full initial glider packet through the
  erase event.
- **Spatial projection:** every projected 5×7 glyph pixel is derived from
  `physical_targets`; no letter shapes come from `ImageFont` inside the
  performance viewport. `ImageFont` is used only for video metadata.
- **Frame-level timing:** the start and end of each lyric word is sampled
  at the video's 24fps boundaries. Error due to the display sampling is at
  most about 41.7ms on top of the previously qualified ≤10.4ms Life phase
  quantization. No claim of phoneme-level accuracy is made.
- **Audio:** video is delivered silent until the song master is supplied.
- **Karaoke completeness:** all **aligned** tokens are represented; Suno's
  background vocalizations without alignment have not yet been timed.
- **No compact physical glider placement:** only video camera presentation
  is compact. Changing physical corridors is separate engineering work.

The draft PR should not be merged until the full-song file actually plays
and the user approves its visual presentation.
