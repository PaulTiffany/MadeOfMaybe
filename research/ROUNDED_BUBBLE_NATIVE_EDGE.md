# Round bubble with prior edge-mounted native turner: measured candidate

This is not a reimplementation of the turner. It reuses the exact same
nineteen-cell B3/S23 beehive/boat/pond station, nine-cell incoming LWSS,
seven-cell original glider eaters and left-side LWSS eater already in the
repository. The station is now near the right edge of a genuine rounded
Life-still-life border at singer-local (925,-125), and the left eater is
near local x=348. The input enters from above through a physical rim gap;
the output flies left in the actual Life universe and is consumed.
The tapered speech-bubble tail consists of stable native 2x2-block cells.
This tail is NOT YET a causally connected cable to the lips.

The new border is an ellipse of radii 350x185 Life cells, sampled onto
a spacing-8 physical still-life lattice. Apertures are physically absent
initial cells, not screen masks. Native ring count is 852 live cells.
The three exact components are 7,902 preserved singer/controller cells,
852 stable ring cells, and 49 original turner/eater/incoming cells:
**8,803 total live g0 cells**.

A native independent-state C++ auditor compared the exact whole world
against the three separately evolved physical components at EVERY Life
generation from g0 through g3648. It found **zero unintended cross-boundary
interactions**. Several tighter rim/tail candidates actually collided at
g341, g451, and g2250 and were rejected; the current candidate passed.
The real controller remains under the bubble and does not yet use the
tail as a glider signal wire, but its already-existing glider corridor is
preserved. The film preserves one continuous affine camera with no hard
cuts, borders, subtitles or renderer-driven mouth poses. It has 457 frames,
24fps at 192 native Life generations per second. The original Suno audio
can be muxed onto this unchanged one-world visual proof.

This makes a nice-sized bubble and a measured staging location for future
seven-row text routing. HOWEVER the precise qualified 36-cell pitch text
is **365x220 for WE**, so the current 700x370 bounding area can show WE
if the inner apparatus is rearranged and verified. It cannot display all
of the physical 403-word train at once: the words must pass through the
bubble and be eaten as the user proposed.

Not yet physically qualified: seven turners inside the rim, 403-word
spacing, a vocal-derived gate from a real isolated stem, the 3-state
reusable mouth oscillator or a lip-control cable through the bubble tail.
The image is an honest one-pixel device placement feasibility proof.
