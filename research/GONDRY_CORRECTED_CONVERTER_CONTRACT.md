# Production correction — the turner MUST remain

The user's intended movie is **not** a stream of already-formed
DOWN-moving words. That was our incorrect interpretation of the
Gondry analogy and must not be promoted into the finished film.

The production design is:

```text
  preloaded LWSS spaceship pixels (g0, far ABOVE)
                        ↓ actual southbound c/2 drift
          genuine B3/S23 19-cell turner
                        ↳ actual native 90° conversion
    moving LWSS letters ← ← ← composing a real word
                        → native row fishhook eats outgoing LWSS
```

This is what the prior `life.native_half_font_first20` **already did**
for a small, real, singer-combined WE at the original Suno word onset
g2156. Preserve that native mechanism, its companion W-to-lip branch,
independent Suno V, rounded bubble, and existing face.

**Use Gondry backward staging at the SOURCE, not the output direction.**
Starting with a desired *leftbound* LWSS pixel (x,y) at exact source
generation G, solve for the 19-cell converter station and its input
collision time, then place the corresponding southbound LWSS far
north at generation zero. Evolve Conway forward normally. The
step is inverse geometry/kinematics of known free-space spaceship
motion, NOT a general inverse of B3/S23 collision.

`life/gondry_corner_backtrace.py` derives this exact formula and
exports real g0 Golly patterns for first two original Suno-aligned
words: WE at g2156 (17 actual turned 3×4 glyph pixels) and MADE at
g2204 (32 actual turned glyph pixels). Each sample retains the
*original 19-cell one-shot converter* per glyph pixel, two qualified
native reaction-glider eaters, and four shared row catchers. CI
independently evolves each complete word world from g0 to g3600,
checks equal native readout at the exact original Suno generation,
verifies whole-world independence before the intended shared catcher,
and checks the final population is only the original persistent eaters.
This is an explicit qualification of conversion, not a 403-word
singer-world qualification.

**Open engineering boundary:** The exact WE and MADE words are
separate physical qualification fixtures, not an unqualified claim
that they simultaneously fit into the same rounded bubble. They are
just 48 generations apart (24 Life cells of westward travel) — a
shared readhead cannot fit both full words in one narrow lane at
36-cell pixel pitch. Integrating all 403 in one bubble requires
source-converter station packing, an intentional continuous
phrase-train spatial design and complete whole-world clearance.
The earlier direct-downward 403-word compiler and its successful
unit/CI tests are retained as a *different valid physical experiment*,
but are explicitly **not the desired production architecture**.

No renderer mouth override, video reversal, disappearing face,
fake native fonts, or one-world equivalence claim is authorized.

## Negative test and physical repair: MADE at g2204

The unmodified 36-cell station mesh **FAILED** actual whole-world B3/S23
component independence at **generation 1521**: converter components 8 and 10
interacted. Uniformly shifting the entire station bank did not fix it.
This disproves a naive promotion of the passing WE layout to arbitrary
miniature words at a much earlier formation deadline.

We kept the same 19-cell native converter, output target and original Suno
clock, and shifted only **MADE input/converter #10** 56 Life cells LEFT.
Its source was simultaneously moved 56 cells NORTH, delaying the actual
conversion by **112 generations** while preserving the expected WESTBOUND
LWSS at g2204. An independent native B3/S23 local replay now has no
unplanned cross-converter interactions through the readout at g2204,
and the intended four-row text catchers consume all incoming ships by
g3600, leaving exactly **476 original 7-cell stationary eaters**.
This is an explicit geometry/timing qualification, not an illusion
produced by reversing playback frames.

The single-word WE fixture starts with 742 real cells and returns to
266 stable eater cells; the MADE fixture starts with 1,372 cells and
returns to 476. These are still **separate single-word worlds**, not
a false claim that both phrases or the full song already share one
physical speech bubble.
