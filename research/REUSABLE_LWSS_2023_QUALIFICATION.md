# Reusable conversion qualification

The previous movie's 19-cell beehive/boat/pond 90-degree LWSS turner is
one-shot, limiting full-song traffic. We evaluated a **published,
existing reusable** 2023 G-to-LWSS converter by Luke Kiernan, using
the original exact 105-cell LifeWiki pattern. After reflecting its
geometry across the x=y diagonal, an incoming SE glider arrives from
above and an output LWSS moves west toward the bubble eater bank.
No new converter was invented; no font pixels are compositor images.

In an exact B3/S23 run, **four** separate input gliders arrived 188
generations apart. At g1000 the complete world equals **exactly the
restored original 105-cell catalyst plus four separated 12-cell-phase
westbound LWSSes**: 153 cells, no ash. A three-source ablation yields
the original catalyst plus only three LWSSes: 141 cells.
This provides an empirically qualified repeatable primitive to pursue
the 403-word stream. The entire 10,366-mini-pixel, multi-rail
high-throughput topology and its actual song-timing/singer integration
remain **unbuilt**. The 188-generation tested repeat interval is
too slow for one lane to carry the whole song; physical lane
multiplexing and recovery scheduling need qualification.
