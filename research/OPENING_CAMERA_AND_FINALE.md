# Opening camera and last-word extinction proposal

## Approved opening art direction

**0–0.75s:** center the actual g0 first source glider and track its native
c/4 SW path at GPS=192. It remains an intact five-cell glider through
generation 144; it first collides with the reusable 84-cell source device
approximately generation 152. These are **world coordinates**, not a visual
glider animation overlaid on a separate circuit.

**0.75–2s:** smoothly shift camera center to the actual 84-cell device
at singer-local center **(382,169.5)** and broaden from 15 to 7.3 screen
pixels per Life cell. Let the actual native collision sell the connection.

**2–11s:** one smooth continuous zoom to the singer + actual bubble +
visible circuitry + native-Life symbolic region, at center **(200,-8)**
and scale **1.055**. First sung WE begins at g2156, ~11.229s.

This specific camera trajectory is stored in
`preview/one_world_camera.py`. It never edits Life cells.
An actual 11s silent reference render was produced from one native
9,286-cell Life initial world with five independent pulsars; the video is
a visual reference, **not** the fully integrated karaoke/lip stage.
The all-song karaoke proof render remains a separate physical universe
and its existing full-screen type presentation must be redesigned to
fit into the native speech bubble.

## Finale concept: NOW. -> real extinction

The last sung line is **RIGHT NOW.**. The last word **NOW.** begins at
g44768 (~233.167s); its karaoke line clears at **g44838 (~233.531s)**.
The 238.88s audio track ends around generation **45865**, leaving ~1027
generations (5.35s) after line-clear to stage a cascading self-destruction.

The intended result is **one final B3/S23 world equal to the empty set**,
not a camera fade, `world.clear()`, negative-time simulation, or global
renderer suppression. Game of Life is not generally reversible. This is
therefore a *destructive unwind*, not mathematical inversion of history.

Candidate strategy: a last-word causal signal initiates a distributed
decommissioning tree. Character/art 2x2 blocks are designed with clean
glider deletion lanes; symbols, lyric blocks, all signal transport, and
their own catalyst/eater infrastructure are dismantled in an ordered wave.
No residual still life or escaping glider is allowed. A final exact
generation replay must establish `population == 0`, and remain zero.
For large distant full-song lyric stages, the spatial light-cone and
time budget require either earlier prepositioned destroyers or a redesigned
compact score; a single glider cannot causally traverse the entire sparse
world in 5s.

**Not implemented or certified.** Existing B3/S23 erasure primitives and
Life's genuinely vanishing Diehard pattern provide useful subassembly
tests, but none establishes whole-universe shutdown by itself.
