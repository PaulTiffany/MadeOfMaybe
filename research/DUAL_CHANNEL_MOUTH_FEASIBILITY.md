# Two real sources for a physically wired three-pose mouth

This feasibility layer treats TWO independent control inputs: W = real Suno
word-onset/word-interval signals, V = activity measured from an already
isolated Suno vocal stem. No lyric source is permitted to impersonate V,
and the full-song mixed audio is not a valid substitute. Word attack
requests OPEN. While V continues, a bounded oscillator alternates
OPEN/MEDIUM even after a word is over. Both signals OFF requests CLOSED.
These are score requests, not rendered edits or proven Game of Life gates.

The existing mouth has three suitable coarse poses (CLOSED/MEDIUM/OPEN);
the real limitation is its physically choreographed glider-bank switching
and re-arming, not the pose vocabulary. Current native mouth pose
completions are commonly separated by about 118-124 generations, or
roughly 0.61-0.65s at the fixed 192 gens/s. The new score conservatively
uses 120-generation dwell as a CANDIDATE; actual reusable mechanism
recovery and collision constraints have NOT yet been certified. 199 of
403 song words last under 0.25s in the alignment, so a separate full
jaw pose on every word is not physically substantiated. Attacks can
nevertheless be true causal signals, even when the slow jaw is unchanged.
An additional tiny fast native jaw-accent mechanism may be useful later.
The dual input logic is not literal phoneme recognition.

Geometry must also be corrected, rather than pretending a camera crop
fits. Current blank Life bubble border bounding box x260..497 (238 wide),
y-174..-33 (142 high). The qualified compact LWSS typewriter pitch is
36 cells: seven glyph rows span 216 cells plus ~4 ship cells, and WE
occupies eleven pixel columns spanning 360+5=365 cells. Thus it cannot
physically fit within the present bubble, even before border/eater
clearance. We need an enlarged rounded still-life bubble or another
independently qualified denser typewriter. The proposed shell will carry
the EXISTING right-angle turners along its top/right aperture, leftward
eaters along its left boundary, and a physically connected control lane
in the tail to the singer. This is an architectural placement target,
NOT an already built native-life scene. Multiword text will travel
through the aperture; longer words must scroll, not teleport or be
deleted by the renderer.

Suno two-stem separation (vocals + instrumental, two takes) costs ten
credits per current tool specification. It has not been triggered
without user approval. Existing MP3 playback does not prove lead-vocal
activity, especially during drums or synthesizers. The synthetic test
activity used to qualify this score is NOT a claim about the song voice.

The code can extract a simple vocal RMS gate from an isolated PCM16 WAV,
with short-gap hysteresis and timestamp quantization. It creates a
traceable C/M/O request timeline from that independent source plus
the original 403-word Suno alignment. Every actual B3/S23 gate, reusable
oscillator, glider fanout, resetting lip bank, and full-world interaction
remains an explicit future native qualification.
