# Moving Conway lyrics: physically eaten by phrase

A native B3/S23 prototype now places two physically moving text segments
**WE MADE** and **A WINDOW** in **one universe**. Each illuminated 5×7 font
pixel is a nine-cell lightweight spaceship (LWSS), travelling left at c/2
(two Life cells per four generations). The text is not renderer-displaced
or generated by placing static 2×2 blocks at playback time.

At the left edge, **eight genuine eater-1 fishhooks** act as the sink:
seven lyric rows and one distinct phrase-marker row. Each reused fishhook
has seven actual cells, so the entire persistent sink is **56 cells**.
The tested orientation swallows one LWSS and returns exactly to its stable
state by generation 40. Repeated ships in a font row pass through **the
same eater**. Exact whole-world Life evolution through generation **1692**
checks all eight lanes against their independently evolved native states.
After both full phrases, their **four real moving START/STOP cue-marker
spaceships**, and all intermediate interactions have passed, the entire
world is **exactly the original 56 eater cells**. No ash or escaping ships.

The physical blank interval is **101 empty horizontal Life cells in the seven lyric rows**
between the phrase segments in the generation-zero seed. The separate cue
lane contains a STOP marker inside that interval, followed by a quiet gap. The code accepts
a `gap_g` parameter in multiples of four, placing actual empty rail space
using the LWSS c/2 motion relationship.

This fits the requested topology:

    [8 eater lanes] <- [WE MADE] [STOP] ...empty gap... [START] [A WINDOW] [STOP]

The START/STOP markers are currently **native physical ships in a dedicated
row**, not yet an electrically/physically connected color switch. A future
switch can use an eater-style detector to set/reset a Life lamp or switch
the paths of subsequent signals; our current renderer's cyan/pink/gold
colors are visualization of cell provenance, NOT a native RGB attribute.

### Limits not to confuse with proof

- This is an *experimental phrase-level train*, not the final Suno-aligned
  403-word mechanism. At the tested 8-cell pitch and c/2 speed, some sung
  word onsets are too close for a single fixed read-head to observe all
  words separately at exact timestamps. An independently routed highlight
  detector or several lanes may be necessary.
- The input ships are preloaded at generation zero, and the entire train
  is visibly moving, not instantly created when sung.
- No actual switch yet connects the cue markers to the singer, phrase
  highlighting, or symbolic animations.
- After phrase consumption, **56 stable eater cells remain**. A true nuke
  ending must explicitly dismantle those too; clearing the camera does
  not count.

Reproduce:
`python -m unittest tests.test_phrase_crawler_eater -v`

The local silent 8.83-second proof movie renders **actual Life-cell
occupancy** at 24 fps, with a fixed, single-world camera and no substituted
letter sprites.
