# Beehive mouth candidate — NOT YET visually accepted

The existing speech bubble, static MADE OF / MAYBE title and pulsar have
not been redesigned. The prior glider-to-block actuator was electrically
functional but failed as a visual mouth: it only blinked one 2x2 dot.

## Better physical primitive

Mitchell Riley's November 2023 *high-clearance glider-to-beehive factory*
can repeatedly toggle a real six-cell hollow beehive.

Source: https://conwaylife.com/wiki/G-to-X_(November_2023)

Adapted one factory from the published two-factory demonstration.
Three gliders are preloaded in generation zero, 48 diagonal cells apart.
The OPEN beehive is present at generations 150 and 540 and absent at
generation 340. The apparatus and face return to exact still lifes.

The isolated face and unchanged bubble plus pulsar were tested together:
the combined B3/S23 world equals union of five independent component
evolutions at every one of 610 consecutive generations.

## Remaining aesthetic obstacle

The six-cell aperture is an improvement over a four-cell block but is
still **small relative to the drawn character**. The prototype face is an
emoji-like sketch and may still read as a blinking dot. Pixel colors are
cosmetic, and close-up preview crops apparatus out of the camera window
without removing it from the underlying simulation.

Do not mark the singer as visually ready. We need a *larger visible
aperture*, likely by compounding several isolated signal-controlled
beehives or by identifying a different larger-state Life transition.
Visual acceptance is separate from these simulator tests.
